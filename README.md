## Features

* Pokémon can evolve immediately after gaining a level during battle.
* Level-up evolutions can still be cancelled with **B**.
* Pokémon on the **bench** can also evolve if they gain a level.
* Compatible with **EXP Share Modes**, including Pokémon that gain EXP without ever entering battle.
* Evolution Stones can be used directly during battle:

  * Fire Stone
  * Water Stone
  * Thunder Stone
  * Leaf Stone
  * Moon Stone
<img width="1026" height="800" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ce722063-7bea-4bce-ad96-e4017e4b92c2" />

* Evolution Stones can be used on both active and benched Pokémon.
* **Rare Candy** can be used during battle.
* After using Rare Candy, the Pokémon is automatically checked for a possible level-up evolution.
* Rare Candy can also be used on benched Pokémon.
* Using an Evolution Stone or Rare Candy consumes the player's turn.
* Evolution keeps the normal Gen1Recomp evolution animation and move-learning flow.
* Moves learned at the evolution level are handled normally after evolution.
* Existing special cases, such as Yellow starter Pikachu refusing a Thunder Stone, are preserved.

## Compatibility

Designed for **Gen1Recomp 0.1.75**.

Includes compatibility with **EXP Share Modes**.
